Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Axshare is a tool for sharing text between machines. This page says what it stores and why.

What you type

The text of a file is stored on the server so it can be synced to everyone who opens the link. Anyone who has the link can read and edit the file. A file is deleted automatically 24 hours after its last edit, or 7 days if it was created while signed in.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with GitHub or Google, Axshare stores your name, your profile picture address, your email address if the provider shares it, and the account identifier the provider gives us. Axshare never sees your password and does not request access to anything else in your account.

Cookies and local storage

When you sign in, a session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. A second short-lived cookie is used only while the sign-in is in progress. The list of recent files on the landing page is kept in your browser's local storage and is not sent to the server. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Logs

The server keeps technical logs of requests, which can include your IP address and the address of the page requested. They are used to operate the service and to limit abuse.

Third parties

Your data is not sold or shared for advertising. The pages load a font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch it. If you sign in, GitHub or Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Files expire on their own. To have your account and its data deleted, write to the address below.
